About Eyeonic

Our story, mission, and commitment to your vision

Home / About Us

Welcome to Eyeonic

Eyeonic is Pakistan's premier destination for high-quality eyewear, offering an extensive collection of frames, sunglasses, contact lenses, and specialty glasses from the world's most trusted brands.

Founded with a passion for optical excellence, we have grown from a small optical shop on Shahrah-e-Faisal, Karachi, into a recognized name in the eyewear industry. Our commitment to quality, style, and customer satisfaction drives everything we do.

Whether you're looking for prescription frames, designer sunglasses, or comfortable contact lenses, Eyeonic has the perfect solution for your vision needs.

Get in Touch

Our Mission

To provide every individual with access to premium eyewear that combines style, comfort, and superior vision correction. We believe that everyone deserves to see the world clearly while looking their best.

  • Offer authentic branded eyewear
  • Provide expert optical guidance
  • Ensure affordable pricing
  • Deliver exceptional customer service

Our Vision

To become the leading eyewear retailer in Pakistan, recognized for our commitment to quality, innovation, and customer satisfaction. We envision a future where premium eyewear is accessible to all.

  • Expand across major cities
  • Launch online shopping platform
  • Partner with global brands
  • Promote eye health awareness

Our Journey

Key milestones in the Eyeonic story

2010
The Beginning

Eyeonic opened its first store on Shahrah-e-Faisal, Karachi, with a vision to bring premium eyewear to Pakistan.

2013
Brand Partnerships

Became authorized dealers for Ray-Ban, Oakley, and Gucci, expanding our product range significantly.

2016
5,000 Happy Customers

Reached our milestone of 5,000 satisfied customers, establishing Eyeonic as a trusted name in eyewear.

2019
Online Presence

Launched our digital platform, making premium eyewear accessible to customers across Pakistan.

2024
10,000+ Customers

Celebrated serving over 10,000 customers with 50+ premium brands and 500+ products in our collection.
